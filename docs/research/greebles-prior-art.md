## 1. Production approaches

Booleaned / welded into hull
- Community workflow order: blockout, primary volumes, panel breaks, boolean cuts, bevel after boolean in stack, weighted normals, then greebles; booleans live until export prep. https://www.claudepluginhub.com/skills/arjun988-blender-skills/hard-surface (single community-authored page; search-summary). Same page: bevel tiers primary 0.005-0.02 m/2-3 seg, panel lines 0.001-0.003 m/1-2 seg, micro 0.0005-0.001 m/1 seg; "instance greebles, keep panels as separate pieces".
- Scifi-Meshes tutorial: Hair-particle-on-collection scatter of ~2000 greeble objects, Convert to real objects, optionally BoolTool Union to one object "if you want geometry bevels to look clean". https://forums.scifi-meshes.com/discussion/10000616/quick-greebling-in-blender (read). No poly counts given.
- Hard Ops/Boxcutter and KIT OPS: live boolean cut/union/intersect inserts, "bake to mesh only when you commit"; KIT OPS Pro authors cutter inserts; free tier places them. https://superhivemarket.com/products/kit-ops-3-pro , https://blenderbros.com/bf-ultimate-guide (search-summary). MESHmachine cleans boolean results via custom normal transfer (Blender Bros bundle page, search-summary).

Floaters / floating geometry
- Polycount (Elite-ship thread, 403 on direct fetch; via search summary): ships "not baked from a single high-poly model... far too big to be custom baked and still retain decent texture resolution"; instead edge loops + custom vertex normals for the smooth high-poly look and "floating decals for livery and numbering". Poster's employer not confirmed. https://polycount.com/discussion/comment/2314421/
- Polycount commenter: keep surface flat and build detail above so it looks indented; good for normal bake, worse where silhouette/shading matter in render. (search-summary) https://polycount.com/discussion/comment/1607703
- Costs of normal-mapped floaters reported: no shadows from bump/normal maps (https://blenderartists.org/t/how-to-add-greeble-to-a-spaceship/455526); greebles baked as normal map distort at grazing angles (POV-Ray news post, https://news.povray.org/pan.2006.06.17.04.52.39.840345%40yahoo.co.uk) (both search-summary).

Decals / trim sheets
- DECALmachine docs: decals are mostly single-polygon meshes; 4 types Simple/Subset/Panel/Info; Simple/Subset/Panel are normal-mapped with a parallax shader effect, works better going into surface than above, one depth level better than several; no UVs needed on host; clipping/flicker if too close to surface (raise via Displace). https://machin3.io/DECALmachine/docs/assets/ , https://machin3.io/DECALmachine (search-summary).
- DECALmachine atlas docs (read): atlas = textures + data.json of positions; atlas and trim sheet share the format; using an atlas rewrites decal UVs so all decals share one Atlas Material (reversible). "HDRP supports deferred rendering... URP supports only forward rendering, and so no decal shaders"; in URP decals are opaque and cannot blend; docs recommend trim sheets instead. Unity parallax matching needs Amplify Shader Editor. Unreal mesh decals cannot be moved in-engine without re-export. https://machin3.io/DECALmachine/docs/export_atlas/ . (Built-in pipeline is not mentioned on that page.)
- Trim sheet pack replicating a Destiny 2 Thrasher: 4 trim sheets x 3 variations (primary paint, secondary paint, metal), material blend masks, decals. https://www.artstation.com/marketplace/p/zAwqK/high-sci-fi-trimsheet-floater-set (search-summary).
- Insomniac Ultimate Trim: see aetheria-hull-materials-prior-art.md:65.

Per-title figures found
- EVE (CCP dev post, undated): high-poly source ~2M+ polys processed in Maya to normal maps for in-game low poly averaging ~5k polys for cruisers; AO baked into diffuse/spec. https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/odd-man-out (search-summary). Camera distance
- Polycount commenters: normal maps common in 1st/3rd person because camera is close, "isometric and top-down usually don't"; a top-down ship dev kept "fat bevels and large details" because units are small on screen; a top-down strategy game pre-rendered very-high-poly ships to sprites (https://polycount.com/discussion/comment/2603097 family; https://www.positech.co.uk/cliffsblog/?p=267; search-summary). Oolite modder: normal maps affect lighting even at distance (https://bb.oolite.space/viewtopic.php?p=265324). Impostors map geometric detail to textures (https://documentation.simplygon.com/SimplygonSDK_9.0.666.0/concepts/impostor.html). Existing repo doc: octahedral-impostors-prior-art.md.
- Size rule from Tripo guide: detail smaller than a few pixels at typical view distance belongs in a texture, not geometry; deep shadow-casting features modelled or height-mapped. https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/explore/hd-model-hard-surface-greeble-design-principles (search-summary, vendor blog).

Authoring time: Scifi-Meshes: booleaning 50 objects ~20 s, 100 ~1 min, author settled on 100-200 per batch. No source gave hours-per-ship numbers.

## 2. Procedural greeble generators

Paper: Kinnear and Kaplan, "Procedural generation of surface detail for science fiction spaceships", Computational Aesthetics 2010. https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/publications/Papers/kinnear_kaplan_2010.pdf (read, all 8 pages). Facts:
- Input is a user-supplied frame of rectangular "nodes" positioned/oriented arbitrarily; any flat surface can be embedded in a rectangle (sec 3, 5). One axis-plane designated bilateral mirror symmetry; rectangles bisected by the plane are split into a symmetric pair (sec 3).
- Pipeline: node -> regular grid cells (3.1) -> randomized T-junction splitting into sub-panels (split point constrained to a scaled centred rectangle; aspect >2.5 forces one-line split; split symmetric with user probability) -> pattern cells (random n-omino, n=4, filled with 6 Wang-tile-like models selected by neighbour labelling, 3.2) -> nurnies: cuboids on 30% of sub-panels, wedges (50%) bridging to neighbour sub-panels, remaining sub-panels filled with a primitive or one of 70 kitbashed library parts, non-uniformly scaled; if x/y scale factors differ >2x, place multiple copies instead (3.3) -> secondary geometry via 7 hard-coded shape-grammar substitution rules (3.4) -> pipes routed on sub-panel edges by a modified Dijkstra spanning tree with straightness penalty (3.5).
- Seams: where two nodes meet at <180 degrees, geometry is added to one side only to avoid interpenetration (3.3).
- Aesthetic claim: details obey frame symmetry in proportion to size/semantic importance; larger more symmetric (2.1.3). Principle "uniformity amidst variety" (2.2).
- Library selection rules: tanks best source; avoid too-recognisable shapes, overly complex geometry, models without roughly flat bottoms (3.3).
- Implementation C++/OpenGL, Houdini used to tear kit parts; output OBJ; AO render. No timings, triangle counts, seeds, or edit-stability discussion. Authors say much work is needed for production (sec 5). Intro: "in many cases the need for efficient rendering relegates detail to painted textures instead of geometry."
- Shape grammar refs in paper: Stiny and Gips 1972; Wonka et al. "Instant architecture" 2003; Mueller et al. 2006 "Procedural modeling of buildings" (CGA shape: staged mass model -> facade -> details; context-sensitive rules to avoid intersections; https://history.siggraph.org/?p=113547). Seed/edit-stability of CGA not found in the sources read.

Blender
- Distribute Points on Faces writes builtin `id`; Random Value and Instance on Points read it implicitly, falling back to index; commit 40c3b8836b7 (Hans Goudey, Oct 2021). https://archive.blender.org/lists/bf-blender-cvs/2021-October/165730.html (read). Manual wording (search-summary): ids stay consistent for remaining points when the mesh is deformed or density changes.
- Reported instability: seeds change for some faces when the source mesh moves (bug T100214, "Needs User Info"); https://developer.blender.org/T100214 (search-summary). Instances sharing a seed are identical copies (blenderartists geometry-node threads).
- Anchoring to face attributes (a persistent per-face id surviving topology edits) was not found in any source; the commit mentions possible future builtins `edge_id`/`face_id`.
- Add-ons: GreebleFX (Blender 2.79-2.9; Basic panels / Advanced nurnies; "best generated when mesh is quad-based"; v1.0) https://superhivemarket.com/products/greeblefx-non-destructive-greeble-generator (read; no seed/anchoring info). "Sci-fi Flats" returned no result.
- Hard Ops/Boxcutter, KIT OPS: manual placement of inserts, not generators.

## 3. Unity built-in runtime options (Unity 2022.3 manual unless stated)

- GPU instancing: same mesh + same material, one draw call; shader must support; built-in pipeline supported; not for SkinnedMeshRenderer; "don't use GPU instancing for meshes with fewer than 256 vertices". https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/GPUInstancing.html (read). Graphics.RenderMeshInstanced calls each issue separate draw calls.
- Priority and exclusion: static batching beats GPU instancing (instancing disabled if static batching applies); instancing disables dynamic batching. https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/optimizing-draw-calls.html (read). Material switching is among the most expensive state changes; Mesh.CombineMeshes gives one draw call.
- Static batching: 64,000 vertices per batch (more -> another batch); meshes must share vertex attributes; extra CPU memory, shared meshes copied per use; after batching move root only; runtime StaticBatchingUtility.Combine available. https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/static-batching.html (read). Manual combine loses per-mesh culling (search-summary of Unity docs).
- Dynamic batching: not for meshes >300 vertices and 900 vertex attributes (pos+normal+UV0 = 300 verts; with UV1+tangent = 180); manual 2020.3-2023.2 (search-summary, https://docs.unity3d.com/2023.2/Documentation/Manual/dynamic-batching.html); Unity 6 pages discourage it for CPU transform cost.
- LOD Group: up to 8 levels, each level a child GameObject; lowest can be billboard; LOD Bias and Maximum LOD Level quality settings. https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/LevelOfDetail.html (read; page does not mention cull-to-zero or screen-relative threshold).
- SRP Batcher applies to SRP only (optimizing-draw-calls page); no built-in-pipeline equivalent described.
- No data found on triangle/draw-call budgets for detail at ARPG distances, or on separate-greeble-mesh vs merged-mesh cost in a many-ship scene (gap). Closest figures: item 1 per-title tri counts.

## 4. Greebles and booleans

- Failure modes reported (Scifi-Meshes read; blenderartists/polycount search-summary): overlapping coplanar faces and bad normals make booleans fail or merge instead of cut; one non-manifold operand can make the whole result garbage; fix cited: Randomize Transform with tiny location/scale jitter on greebles; batch operands in 10-200 groups. https://forums.scifi-meshes.com/discussion/10000616/quick-greebling-in-blender
- Bevel: bevel-generated faces take neighbours' pre-bevel vertex normals, hence Weighted Normal; bevel corner streaks on curved holes, fix by limiting bevel weight to boolean edge loops only; Houdini user: "black triangles with boolean+bevel", suspected tessellation. https://blenderartists.org/t/boolean-and-bevel-on-curved-surface/1308394 , https://www.sidefx.com/forum/post/221668/ (search-summary). Merge small greebles into one object before geometry bevel (claudepluginhub page above).
- Blender solvers (Boolean modifier manual 4.5): Exact is robust, "much slower"; Hole Tolerant and Self Intersection options cost more; Manifold solver (4.5) as fast as/faster than Float but manifold inputs only. https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/modeling/modifiers/generate/booleans.html ; forum https://devtalk.blender.org/t/manifold-boolean-feedback/40150 (search-summary). A vendor claim: converting meshes to instances before booleans gives up to 100x (Float) / 10x (Exact) speedup (blendernation listing, creator claim, unverified). No many-operand benchmark found.
- Parenting vs booleaning: no report found of shipping games booleaning greebles into the hull; common published practice is booleans for cuts/panel breaks, instanced/separate pieces for repeated greebles (claudepluginhub, Tripo guide, Scifi-Meshes). Kinnear and Kaplan place greeble geometry as separate cuboids/parts (no boolean) and avoid interpenetration by one-side placement.
- UV pressure: floaters/decals rely on atlas/trim or normal bake (DECALmachine atlas; Elite-style rebuild's 2048 maps; EVE bake). Only the polycount Elite statement above addresses a whole-hull bake being too large for texture resolution. No source found on atlas UV cost of unique-per-ship livery plus greeble bake.
